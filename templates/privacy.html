Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter into the app.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited and actions taken, used to improve the product.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely to provide and improve Spendly. Specifically:

  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To store and display your expense records accurately.
  • To generate summaries and insights within your dashboard.
  • To communicate important service updates or security notices to you.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside of delivering the Service to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon request, we will permanently remove your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may use trusted third-party services (such as hosting providers) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements. These providers are not permitted to use your data for any purpose other than delivering their service to us.

We do not embed third-party advertising networks, social media trackers, or analytics services that would share your data with outside parties.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses session cookies strictly to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this will prevent you from staying signed in.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via the contact information provided on our website. We will respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 7 business days.
